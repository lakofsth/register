## The instinct to build

When you name a new part, ask: *what is this doing, in the real activity this project models?*
Name it that. If a word from another world (contest, security, courtroom, warfare, medicine) is
the first that comes to mind, it's usually describing the mechanism's shape, not its purpose
here — reach past it for the word a person would use for the same move in the real domain.
